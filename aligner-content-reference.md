# Aligner content reference

How this site's aligner education content is structured, and where the
pillars come from.

## Source note
The site-crawling tools are gated on this workspace, so the ten brand sites
(Invisalign, Spark, ClearCorrect, SureSmile, Angel, 3M Clarity, Henry Schein
Reveal, AlignerCo, Argen ISON, TP Orthodontics) could not be fetched
directly. The education content below follows the industry-standard patient
education pillars those brands share, written in our own words with no
trademarked names, slogans, statistics or claims copied from any of them.

If specific text should be mirrored more closely, paste it and it can be
adapted into the same tone.

## Education pillars applied

1. How aligners work (mechanics):
   gentle constant pressure, bone remodeling, sequential trays that lock in
   each stage of movement. Section: "How trays move teeth" on /product.

2. Daily routine (lifestyle):
   wear 20-22 hours, remove for eating and cleaning, brush trays gently with
   mild soap, no hot water, store in the travel case. Section: "Life with
   aligners" on /product. Wear guidance echoed in the dentist clinical
   checklist on /dentists.

3. Treatment process:
   3D scan, dentist-mapped plan, tray changes every one to two weeks,
   halfway check-in, completion, then retainers. Sections: "Scan to smile in
   four steps", "A typical course, week by week" on /product; "Plan your
   visit" on /experience-centers.

4. Candidacy:
   mild to moderate crowding, small gaps, minor rotations, relapse; severe
   cases and bite problems assessed at a consult. Section: "Is this course
   for you?" on /product.

5. Common questions:
   invisibility, duration, discomfort, eco material, eating and drinking,
   cleaning, attachments, retainers, teenagers, sports, tray-change
   cadence. Full set on /product ("Asked before the first scan"), first five
   on the home page ("Questions, answered").

6. Comparisons:
   clear aligners vs fixed braces on appearance, eating, comfort, check-ins
   and eco footprint, kept qualitative. Section: "Aligners, or fixed
   braces?" on the home page.

## Rules kept
- No copied slogans, logos, trademarked product names or brand-specific
  statistics (no "9 out of 10", no "X% faster", no material trademarks like
  SmartTrack or TruFlex).
- No invented marketing performance claims; only generic, accurate,
  industry-standard guidance (wear hours, cleaning, retention) and the
  studio's own plausible product specs.
