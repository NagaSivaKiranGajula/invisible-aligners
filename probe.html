Questions, answered

The things every new patient asks before their first visit.

Will people notice I am wearing aligners?+

No. The trays are virtually invisible: crystal clear with a matte finish that blends with your smile. Most patients say nobody spots them.

How long does a full course take?+

Mild to moderate cases typically run 6 to 12 months. Your dentist maps the exact number of trays at the planning visit.

Do the trays hurt?+

You feel gentle pressure for a day or two after each new tray, then it eases. The studio is known for a pain-free, patient-first approach.

How is this eco-friendly?+

Our trays are made from a biodegradable polymer (ASTM D6400) that breaks down in commercial composting, with zero-waste, recyclable packaging.

How do I start?+

Book a consultation. We scan your smile, show you a digital simulation of the result, and your first tray is ready within days.
